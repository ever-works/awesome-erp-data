## Overview

Invoice Ninja is a free and open-source ERP software platform designed for invoicing, accounting, and business management. It is written in PHP and supports self-hosted deployment.

## Features

- Invoicing and billing
- Accounting and expense tracking
- Client management
- Payment gateway integrations
- Recurring invoices
- Self-hosted deployment
- Open-source

## Pricing

Free and open-source self-hosted version. Cloud-hosted plans available (pricing on website).
