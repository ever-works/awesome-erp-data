## Overview

Katana is an ERP system explicitly designed for SMBs in the manufacturing industry, standing out for inventory management. While technically ERP software that connects processes across organizational departments, it functions closer to material requirements planning (MRP) software with ERP functionality.

## Features

- Robust live inventory management capabilities
- Visual and intuitive interface that is easy to use
- Omnichannel sales order management
- Outsourced purchase tracking
- Automatic stock refactoring
- Time tracking, scheduling, and task prioritization
- Cloud accounting and expense insights
- Multi-location inventory tracking
- Open API and no-code ecosystem for custom workflows
- Third-party integrations via Zapier
- Integrates with popular eCommerce platforms and marketplaces

## Pricing

Paid. Specific pricing varies by plan and features.

## Deployment

Cloud and on-premise options available.

## Considerations

- Not ideal for industries that don't rely on physical inventory
- Lacks comprehensive functionalities for business areas like accounting and HR
- Generous implementation assistance available for rollout
- High marks for customer service and ease of onboarding
