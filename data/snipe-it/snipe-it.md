## Overview

Snipe-IT is an open source IT asset management system that helps organizations track hardware and software assets, manage equipment bookings and checkouts, prevent double-bookings, and record custody and audit history across multiple locations.

## Features

- Asset lifecycle tracking (purchase, assignment, maintenance, retirement)
- Equipment booking and checkout with double-booking prevention
- Custody tracking and audit trails across locations
- License and software asset management
- Consumables and accessories tracking
- Location and department management
- Reporting and audit history
- REST API for integrations
- Self-hosted deployment

## Pricing

Free and open source under the AGPL-3.0 license. Self-hosted; community support available.
