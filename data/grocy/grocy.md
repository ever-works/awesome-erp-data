# grocy

grocy is an ERP-style household and grocery management solution for tracking food inventory, shopping lists, chores, and household tasks.

## Features

- Food and product inventory management
- Shopping list generation and tracking
- Chore and task management
- Expiration date tracking
- Recipe and meal planning
- Barcode scanning support
- User and household management

## Deployment

Self-hosted via PHP and Docker.
