# farmOS

farmOS is a web-based farm record keeping application designed for managing farm operations including crops, livestock, equipment, and financials.

## Features

- Crop and field record keeping
- Livestock and animal management
- Equipment and asset tracking
- Financial logging and activity tracking
- Map-based field and location management
- Event and task logging

## Deployment

Self-hosted via PHP and Docker.
