## Overview

OpenConstructionERP is an open-source construction ERP platform with BOQ, PDF/CAD/BIM takeoff, AI cost matching, and 293 guided cases. It includes 120,000+ priced positions, 44 languages, 190+ modules, and cost data for 40+ markets.

## Features

- Open-source construction ERP
- BOQ (Bill of Quantities)
- PDF/CAD/BIM takeoff
- AI cost matching
- 293 guided cases
- 120,000+ priced positions
- 44 languages
- 190+ modules
- Cost data for 40+ markets
- AGPL-3.0 license
